I have a tendency to tweak projects in my head whenever I am sick, I can’t write, or when I get back from a trip. All three happened this past month, so here we are.

Nuance Node is no more, at least in this iteration. This is a delayed effect of the [hard turn](https://tbhpress.substack.com/p/taking-a-hard-turn-with-no-destination) that I took in March. While I do still believe in the original vision to build a digital space that is focused on the sparse art of nuance, it became a bit limiting. I began to look at my website name and twinge. It became a fly that buzzed around me as I sat at my desk. I need my page to be a place where I can write a bit of everything. TBH Press is that.
