I have a tendency to tweak projects in my head whenever I am sick, I can’t write, or when I get back from a trip. All three happened this past month, so here we are.

Nuance Node is no more, at least in this iteration. This is a delayed effect of the [hard turn](https://tbhpress.substack.com/p/taking-a-hard-turn-with-no-destination) that I took in March. While I do still believe in the original vision to build a digital space that is focused on the sparse art of nuance, it became a bit limiting. I began to look at my website name and twinge. It became a fly that buzzed around me as I sat at my desk. I need my page to be a place where I can write a bit of everything. TBH Press is that.

Nothing should change for you as the reader. I am still writing and sharing my thoughts here. Consider this to be just another messy aspect of establishing a creative process. *Oh, I have one of those now.* I even submitted something that I wrote to some mean editor someplace who surely won’t like it. Progress.

I’ll be changing the domain name and a few other fiddly bits to make this change obvious to any new subscribers coming in. To anyone sad about this, don’t fret. It is likely that I pick up the Nuance Node project again at some point down the road. I know a few of you are fans of the idea. We do still need more nuance in the world—I just think you need something grander than a Substack to make an impact.

I am sure that there will be some other changes on the way but that’s it for now. I hope you don’t mind the confusion. **You can punish me by not liking my posts, but you do still have to open them whenever possible.** Thanks for reading!
